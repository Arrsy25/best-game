EMBERKEEPER

defend the last lantern

Night has swallowed the arena, and one small hooded keeper holds the last light. Your lantern is your life, your ammo and your vision — it drains as you stand, and the shades outside its glow are coming for it.

Slay the shades, gather their embers, chain kills into a combo, choose a blessing between waves, and survive all 10 waves — until the Night Lord falls and dawn breaks.
