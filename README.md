**Emberkeeper — defend the last lantern.** A top-down arena survival game
built for the [make.fun best-game-01](https://make.fun/c/best-game-01)
competition. You are a small hooded keeper with a lantern in a dark arena.
Your light is your life, your ammo and your vision: it drains as you stand,
so slay the shades that drift in from the dark, gather the embers they drop,
and keep the flame alive through 10 waves — choosing a blessing between
waves — until the Night Lord falls and dawn breaks.

- WASD / arrows to move, mouse to aim, hold click to fling an ember bolt
  (each shot costs a little light), Space to dash, P to pause, M to mute.
- Combo kills raise a score multiplier; a hit resets it.
- All art is drawn procedurally on canvas and all sound is synthesised
  live with WebAudio. No libraries, no network, no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and press Enter to play.
